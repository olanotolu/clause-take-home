EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Stark Dynamics Inc. ("Employer") and the undersigned employee ("Employee").

1. Confidentiality

1.1 Employee shall not disclose Employer's Confidential Information during or after employment, and shall use it solely for Employer's benefit.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include Employee's general skills, knowledge and experience, or information that is publicly available.

2. Inventions

2.1 Employee assigns to Employer all inventions conceived or developed within the scope of employment.

2.2 Employee shall promptly disclose to Employer each such invention.

3. Return of Property

3.1 On termination of employment, Employee shall return all Employer property and Confidential Information.

4. Whistleblower Protections

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws.
