MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Hooli Holdings ("Company") and Pied Piper Consulting ("Vendor").

1. Scope

1.1 Vendor shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work.

2. Confidential Information

2.1 Vendor shall not disclose Company's Confidential Information and shall use it solely to perform the services.

2.2 Company shall not disclose Vendor's pricing or methodologies to any third party without Vendor's consent.

3. Data Protection

3.1 Where Vendor processes Personal Data on behalf of Company, Vendor shall do so only on documented instructions from Company and in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum.

4. Insurance

4.1 Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence.

5. Indemnification

5.1 Vendor shall indemnify Company against losses arising from Vendor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.2 Upon termination, Vendor shall return or destroy Company's Confidential Information.
