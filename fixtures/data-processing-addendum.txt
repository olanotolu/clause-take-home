DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the agreement between Soylent Data Corp ("Processor") and Wonka Retail Group ("Controller").

1. Roles

1.1 Controller determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data. Processor processes Personal Data only on Controller's documented instructions.

2. Confidentiality of Personnel

2.1 Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

3. Security

3.1 Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data, including encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Sub-processors

4.1 Processor shall not engage a sub-processor without Controller's prior written authorisation and shall impose equivalent data protection obligations on each sub-processor.

5. Breach Notification

5.1 Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

6. Deletion

6.1 At the end of the services, Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data at Controller's choice.
