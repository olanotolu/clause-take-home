## Design Context

### Users
In-house counsel and legal operations teams reviewing contracts, finding clauses, and moving proposed edits through a careful validation workflow. Search should get them to the right document or verified passage quickly, without making them wonder whether a result is trustworthy.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and capable. The interface should feel focused and dependable during long work sessions. Use plain, direct language and avoid decorative AI cues.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the CLAUSE product register in `DESIGN.md`: neutral surfaces, hairline separation, one restrained blue accent, and status colors only when they carry meaning. Keep the light workspace as the default visual reference and retain the existing dark theme. Avoid generic command-center styling, heavy blur, gratuitous gradients, and repeated icon cards.

### Design Principles
1. Make document identity and passage context unmistakable.
2. Keep the common keyboard path fast and free of decorative motion.
3. Separate title matches from clause-text matches, with clear next actions.
4. Show verified search snippets as source material, not as AI-generated advice.
5. Keep contrast, focus, and reduced-motion support intact.
