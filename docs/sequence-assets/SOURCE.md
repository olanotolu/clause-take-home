The text was extracted with `pdftotext -layout`; layout and some scanned or encoded text may not be faithful to the PDF. Consult the source for authoritative wording. Section 4.2 appears on packet page 71 (agreement page 6).

The local working copy is for demonstrating search and a proposed change. No claim is made that an edit changes the public agreement, that the agreement was executed, or that any insurance coverage exists. The saved exact-text requirement is a demonstration rule, not an approved playbook from the parties. Clause CI's findings are advisory.
